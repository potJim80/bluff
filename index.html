Games

Open a game on the big screen. Everyone plays from their phone.

Bluff
Put cards down face down and claim the rank. Call it if you think they're lying.
2–10 players · turns
Corner
Everyone trades at once, shouting numbers. First to hold all nine of one good corners the market.
3–8 players · all at once
Kemps
Collect four of a kind and signal your partner without a word. Get caught and it's theirs.
4, 6 or 8 players · teams of two
Durak
Attack, defend, pile on. Nobody wins — the last one holding cards is the fool.
2–6 players · attack and defend
